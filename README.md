# 📱 GCT BWP App

**GCT BWP App** is an Android application developed by **Adeeb Technology Lab** that brings essential information about **Government College of Technology, Bahawalpur (GCT BWP)** directly to mobile users.  
This educational app provides offline access to college details, departmental information, schedules, admission data, syllabus info, and more — helping students and visitors quickly find what they need on the go.

---

## 📍 About

**GCT BWP** is one of Southern Punjab’s leading technical colleges, providing programs including Diploma of Associate Engineering (DAE) and other technical education tracks. This app aims to make college data accessible without needing continuous internet access by packaging it into a mobile‑friendly format. 

The app is designed to help:

- 🎓 Students access academic details (departments, courses, fees)  
- 📋 Visitors find general college information  
- 📱 Users retrieve information offline without internet

The project is part of **Adeeb Technology Lab’s** initiative to build mobile utilities that support students and learning communities.
